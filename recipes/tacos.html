Classic Street Tacos

Description

These classic street tacos are packed with flavor and perfect for any occasion. Made with seasoned ground beef, fresh vegetables, and a zesty sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef until browned.
  2. Add the taco seasoning and water according to package directions.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes until thickened.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  5. Fill each tortilla with the beef mixture and top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and sour cream.
