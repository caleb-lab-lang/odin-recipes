Classic Backyard Burgers

Description

These classic backyard burgers are juicy, flavorful, and perfect for grilling season. Made with ground beef, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced pickles
  • 1/2 cup sliced onions
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 1/4 cup mustard
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat your grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape them into patties. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Grill the patties for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness.
  4. During the last minute of grilling, place a slice of cheese on each patty and close the grill lid to melt the cheese.
  5. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill for about 1 minute until lightly browned.
  6. Assemble the burgers by placing the patties on the buns and adding your favorite toppings such as pickles, onions, ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise.
  7. Serve immediately with your favorite sides like fries or a salad.
