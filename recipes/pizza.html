Homemade Pizza

Description

A delicious homemade pizza with a crispy crust and flavorful toppings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup sliced pepperoni

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine warm water, yeast, and sugar. Let sit for 5 minutes until foamy.
  2. Add olive oil and half of the flour. Mix until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise for 1 hour or until doubled.
  5. Punch down the dough and roll it out on a floured surface to fit your pizza pan.
  6. Transfer the dough to the pan and add the tomato sauce and cheese.
  7. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown.
